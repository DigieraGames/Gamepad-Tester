Everything you need to check a controller

One app, six checks, no setup. Connect a controller and press a button.

🎮

Live gamepad view

Both sticks with L3 and R3 clicks, D-pad, A/B/X/Y, bumpers, triggers with percentages and Select/Start/Mode, all on one screen. Switch between Xbox and PlayStation labels.

◎

Stick drift test

Let go of the sticks and the app measures how far each one wanders on its own, gives a plain verdict and recommends a deadzone.

⭕

Stick range test

Roll each stick around its edge and watch the shape fill in. If a direction never fills, the stick isn't reaching it.

▦

Button checks

Press counts for every button, double-press (bounce) detection, stuck-button warnings and a mash test to see how fast you can go.

📈

Trigger curves

Pull a trigger slowly and see the curve. A good one rises smoothly to 100%. A worn one jumps or never gets there.

Rumble tests

Questions

Which controllers work?

Most controllers that Android recognises over Bluetooth or USB (use a USB-C OTG adapter for wired ones). That includes Xbox and PlayStation pads and many third-party controllers. Whether vibration, battery and motion sensors show up depends on your controller and your Android version.

My controller isn't showing up

Pair it in your phone's Bluetooth settings (or plug it in with an OTG adapter), then open the app and press any button. If it still doesn't appear, check that the controller works in another app and try a different cable or adapter.

Does it need an internet connection?

All the tests work offline. Ads, and checking the Remove Ads purchase, need a connection.

Is it free? How do I remove the ads?

The app is free and shows a few small ads: a banner on some screens, one when the app opens and an occasional one after a game. Never on the gamepad screen or while you play. A one-time purchase in Settings removes all ads for good.

What data does the app collect?

Your controller data stays on your phone. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID and similar data. Everything is explained in the privacy policy.

Got a controller that behaves oddly?

Run the tests and you'll know in a minute.
