Privacy Policy for Gamepad Tester

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Gamepad Tester ("the app") is a tool for testing game controllers, with a small built-in game. It is free and supported by advertising.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (theme, button labels, deadzone and similar options) and your high score are saved locally on your phone.
  • Controller input (button presses, stick positions, device name and IDs) is read only to show it on screen. It is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • If you tap "Share test report", the app creates a text summary and hands it to Android's share sheet. It is only sent where you choose to send it.

The app has no accounts, and we (the developer) do not receive or store any of your data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a small banner on some screens, an ad when the app opens, and an occasional full-screen ad after a game ends (never on the gamepad view or during play). The one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns all of them off.

To show ads, Google's AdMob SDK may collect and use data from your device, such as:

  • your advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • app interaction, diagnostics and device information (model, OS version, language).

Google uses this to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and some other places), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads). More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Remove ads") through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you own the purchase, so it can hide ads, and remembers that result on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
